I need a Go component for running child processes. It needs to cap stdout and
stderr so a noisy child cannot consume unlimited memory; when a stream reaches
the cap, keep what fits, drop the rest and report the truncation instead of
failing the run. It must support both a timeout and caller cancellation, and
both must stop the child process and anything it spawned. I need stdout and
stderr kept separate, and the result must distinguish failure to start,
non-zero exit, timeout and cancellation. It has to run on Linux, macOS and
Windows.
